# Snipstow

Clipboard history for your Mac, in the menu bar. Everything stays on your
Mac: no account, no subscription.

**Website:** [https://ayushpratap27.github.io/Snipstow-releases/](https://ayushpratap27.github.io/Snipstow-releases/)

## Download

Get the latest **Snipstow-x.y.z.dmg** from
[Releases](https://github.com/ayushpratap27/Snipstow-releases/releases/latest).

Needs a Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or later) and macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

## Install

1. Open the disk image and drag **Snipstow** onto **Applications**.
2. Open Snipstow. The first time, macOS says it can't verify the app: click
   **Done**, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click
   **Open Anyway** next to Snipstow. Open it again and click **Open**.
3. When you first pick a clip, allow Snipstow under **Accessibility** so it
   can paste for you.

## Updates

Snipstow checks for updates once a day and tells you when one is ready; you
can also right-click its menu-bar icon and choose **Check for Updates…**.
Updates install with one click, and you won't see the "can't verify" step
again.
